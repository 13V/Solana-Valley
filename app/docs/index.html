📖 Overview

Farm Lands is a wholesome farming game in the spirit of Stardew Valley, crossed with a "Grow a Garden"-style rare-crop economy. You're given your own plot in a shared valley: till the soil, plant seeds, water your crops, and harvest them in real time. Rarer crops and lucky mutations are worth dramatically more — chasing them is the heart of the game.

The design splits cleanly into play-off-chain / own-on-chain: the cozy moment-to-moment farming runs instantly and for free in your browser, while the things you truly own and trade — the $SPROUT token, rare items, and the player marketplace — settle on Solana.

🌾 Deep farming loop

19 plants across 8 rarity tiers, value-multiplying mutations, a restocking seed shop, and 5 levelling skills.

🐔 A living homestead

Chickens, cows and fruit trees produce passive income — and breed, so your herd grows itself.

🪙 Real ownership

$SPROUT, a marketplace, and revenue-funded rewards — designed to be sustainable, never a Ponzi.

🚀 Quick start Live

  1. Open the game at sproutvalley.fun (or hit ▶ Play). It runs right in your browser — no install.
  2. Find ★ Your Plot. You farm your own allocated plot in a valley of 20; the camera follows you as you explore.
  3. Run the loop: Hoe the grass → plant a Seed → Water it → wait for it to ripen → click to harvest → sell in the Harvest panel.
  4. (Optional) Connect a wallet. Phantom or Solflare on devnet shows your address & live SOL balance. Nothing here touches real funds yet.

🔁 The core loop Live

Everything orbits one satisfying cycle:

🪓 Hoe  →  🌱 Plant  →  💧 Water  →  ⏳ Grow  →  🧺 Harvest  →  🪙 Sell  →  ⬆️ Reinvest

  • Watering doubles growth speed, and watered crops earn a Wet bonus (×1.5 value) at harvest. Rain waters every tilled tile for free.
  • Selling crops earns coins (spend on seeds, upgrades, animals) and XP (level up to unlock rarer seed tiers).
  • Reinvest into upgrades and rarer seeds — the loop compounds as you climb the rarity ladder.

🎣 Fishing Live

Cast at the pond (fresh water) or the open sea (salt water) and play the one-click cast → bite → hook minigame: the rod arcs out, the bobber settles, bubbles rise when a fish is interested, then the bobber dunks — click the moment it does to set the hook. Miss the window and it gets away.

Fish run the same 8-tier rarity ladder as crops, from everyday Minnows up to Celestial white-whales like the Leviathan Eel. The Fishing skill's luck tilts the odds toward rarer catches, and the ocean holds bigger, more valuable species than the pond.

🚶 Explore mode Live

Maps you build can be walked around inside the game. Press M in the farm (or open the game with ?map=<name>) to drop into a standalone, walkable view of an editor-built map.

  • Move with WASD / arrow keys; the camera follows and the world is bounded to your map.
  • Collision is automatic: tiles on any layer above the ground layer (fences, water, buildings…) block you — so build terrain on the bottom layer, obstacles on top.
  • Play your own: drag a sprout-map.json exported from the editor onto the window to load it instantly.
  • Press Esc (or M again) to return to the farm.
🧭
Explore mode is fully separate from your farm — it never touches your save, crops or progress. It's a sandbox for showing maps off.

🪙 $SPROUT tokenomics Rolling out

The core principle

Pay out real yield, never printed yield.

Every reward is funded by money that actually came in — marketplace fees, cosmetic sales, and treasury staking yield. The reward pool can only distribute what it holds, so it can never death-spiral. Rewards are funded only by real revenue — not by new-player inflows (Ponzi) or token emissions (inflation).

🌾 Community Harvest Pool Roadmap

A single, transparent, on-chain pool that self-balances — it pays out only what it takes in each season.

Inflows (real revenue)

A share of marketplace fees · a share of cosmetic/premium-seed sales · treasury staking yield (never principal) · the "harvest tax" sink.

Outflows (capped ≤ inflows)

Seasonal rewards · quests & bounties · referral rewards · a small slice of SOL top prizes.

The rule: season payout ≤ pool balance. If revenue dips, payouts shrink — the system bends, it never breaks.

Rewards favour contribution, not hours

Paying per hour invites bots and ruins the cozy vibe. Instead, each season the pool splits by a contribution score built from things bots can't cheaply fake: Almanac discoveries, collection/quality milestones, achievements, and decorated-farm "likes" from other players — with only modest, diminishing-returns weight on raw volume.

🍯
The honest takeaway: sustainable play-to-earn here is a cozy bonus + seasonal prizes, not a wage. It scales with the game — more trading and spend means a bigger pool. We will never print or Ponzi to inflate it.

🔒 Staking — the Garden Vault Rolling out

Lock $SPROUT in the Garden Vault for a cozy, tiered boost to your farm — and to qualify for the seasonal reward pool. Bigger stakes and longer locks earn bigger boosts.

TierStakeBoost (stacks with skills & upgrades)

  • Time-lock + unstake cooldown — longer locks earn bigger multipliers; the cooldown blocks flash-loan/flip gaming. Locking supply is also a gentle sink that supports the token.
  • Staking = reward-eligibility — the same lock that boosts your farm also makes you eligible for seasonal rewards, which doubles as Sybil resistance (fake accounts cost more than they earn).
  • Boosts stay modest — a cozy bonus, never a pay-to-win wall.
⚠️

🛒 Marketplace Roadmap

A player-to-player marketplace for rare crops, mutations and items — priced in $SPROUT and settled by an on-chain escrow program (list, fill, cancel). This turns the rare-crop chase into a real, player-driven economy.

A small marketplace fee is the main engine of the Community Harvest Pool: trading activity funds the rewards that flow back to engaged players. Wash-trading and self-dealing are excluded from fee-funded reward scoring.

🛡️ Fair play & anti-cheat Roadmap (M2)

Honesty first: the game is 100% client-side today (your save lives in your browser). That's perfect for a free single-player game — and it means no real value can be attached to it yet, because a client-trusted save could be edited or botted. Making value-minting trustworthy is a hard prerequisite for any withdrawable reward.

Before $SPROUT/SOL is ever on the line, the architecture moves to an authoritative server + on-chain settlement hybrid: the client predicts and renders, but the server owns the clock, the RNG and the economy, and only it (and the chain) can credit rewards.

ThreatMitigation
Save tamperingServer/chain is the source of truth; the local save is a cache only
Clock / time-warpServer owns time; growth is computed from server timestamps
RNG manipulation (re-rolling mutations)Server-seeded RNG (commit-reveal) or on-chain VRF for high-value rolls
Botting & SybilRate limits, per-wallet/season caps, stake-to-earn, contribution scoring
Wash tradingGraph/heuristic detection; self-dealing excluded from rewards

😌The free, single-player cozy mode stays client-side forever. Only the rewarded / on-chain mode uses the authoritative path, so the cozy experience is never burdened by latency.

❓ FAQ

Is the game free to play?

Yes. The entire cozy farming loop is free and runs in your browser. $SPROUT, the marketplace and rewards are an optional layer at the edges — you never have to spend to enjoy the game.

Do I need crypto or a wallet to play?

No. You can play the full game with no wallet at all. Connecting a Solana wallet (devnet) is optional and currently just shows your address and balance.

Is it pay-to-win?

No. Boosts from staking and cosmetics are deliberately modest, and progression is driven by playing — not by buying power. Rewards favour contribution (discoveries, quality, collections), not raw spend or grind.

How do players actually earn real value?

Through the revenue-funded Community Harvest Pool: a share of marketplace fees, cosmetic sales and treasury yield is paid out each season, split by contribution. It's a cozy bonus, not a salary — and it can only ever pay out what it took in.

What stops bots from draining the rewards?

Bots can farm free off-chain coins all they like — that's worthless. The reward layer is server/chain-authoritative with per-wallet caps, contribution scoring, stake-to-earn and bot/wash-trade detection. See fair play.

When does $SPROUT / staking / the marketplace go live?

See the roadmap. These roll out in stages; staking and the marketplace require the token mint and authoritative anti-cheat to be in place first.

Where do I find the official contract address?

Only from our official website and verified social channels. Treat any address DMed to you as a scam.

Will my farm be wiped?

Your single-player save persists locally. As on-chain features and cloud saves arrive, we'll communicate any migrations clearly in advance.
